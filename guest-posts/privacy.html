Guest Posts: Write for Us privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Guest Posts: Write for Us ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that lets visitors send guest posts through a form, checks each post with AI and lets the site owner publish good posts to the site's blog. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from visitors who send a guest post

  • The name, email address, post title and post text the author types, the blog category they pick and, if they add one, a cover image. Before upload the image is made smaller in the author's browser, which also removes metadata such as the location where it was taken.
  • An anonymous visitor id that Wix gives to every site visitor (or the member id, if the author is logged in as a site member), the language of the page and the time of sending. The app uses them to limit how often one visitor can send posts and to write the AI note and emails in the right language. The app does not ask for or store phone numbers or IP addresses.
  • The author name, title and text of each post are sent for an AI review through the AI service built into the Wix platform (Wix AI APIs), together with the blog topic the site owner set. The review is paid for with the site's AI credits; the email address and cover image are not sent. The AI returns a verdict, a list of issues and a short note for the site owner.
  • Posts, their AI review and their status are stored in data collections of the app on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours. Cover images are stored in a Guest Posts folder of the site's Media Manager. Only the site owner and the site's collaborators see submissions in the dashboard; visitors see a post only after the site owner publishes it to the blog, with the author's name, title, text and cover. The email address is never published.
  • Submissions that look automated, such as a filled hidden field or a form sent within seconds, are discarded and not stored.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your settings in the same site's data collection: the AI check and blog topic, automatic publishing, form options, blog categories, blocked words, look options, notification choices and the language of your dashboard for emails.
  • To publish a post, the app creates a post or a draft in your site's blog (Manage Blog permission). The blog needs a site member as the author, so the app reads site members (Read Members permission) to use the author of your latest blog post, your own member profile or the earliest member of the site.
  • To review posts with AI, the app calls the AI service of the Wix platform on behalf of your site (Invoke AI Models permission).
  • To tell you about new posts, the app reads the site owner's email (Read Site Owner Email permission) and sends an email through Wix (Manage Email Marketing permission), plus a notification in your dashboard and the Wix app (Manage Notifications permission). The email does not include the author's name, title or text. You can turn each off in the settings.
  • Cover images are uploaded to and removed from your Media Manager (Manage Media Manager permission).
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know whether the form should show on the live site, and keeps only whether the plan is active and when it was checked.
  • If you ask Aria about your guest posts, the app returns counts of posts and your settings to Aria inside Wix, never names, emails or texts. Aria can only read, not change anything.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising. As the site owner, you decide what to publish and are responsible for telling your visitors how you use their posts and contact details.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. The AI review runs through the Wix platform's AI service, which may use AI model providers engaged by Wix. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Submissions stay in the app until the site owner deletes them in the app dashboard; deleting a submission also removes its cover image, unless the post is already in the blog. Posts published or saved as drafts live in the site's blog and are managed there. An author who wants a post or their details removed can ask the site owner. When the site owner uninstalls the app, Wix may keep or delete the app's data collections under its own rules; posts already in the blog and images in the Media Manager stay until the site owner deletes them. To ask a question about your data, email us.
