VAT Toggle: Prices Excl VAT privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

VAT Toggle: Prices Excl VAT ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that lets visitors see store prices including or excluding VAT. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from visitors

  • The switch runs inside the visitor's browser. Your settings come with the page itself, and prices are recalculated on the page; the app sends no requests about visitors and does not collect or store their names, emails, IP addresses or any other personal data.
  • The visitor's choice, prices with or without VAT, is kept only in their own browser's local storage, so it stays as they browse and on their next visit from the same device. It is never sent to us or to the site owner, and the visitor can clear it in their browser settings.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores only your settings (VAT rate, how your prices are entered, what new visitors see first, the switch corner and color, and your own labels) in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours. Only the app's server can read this collection.
  • The app reads basic site information and the app plan from Wix to know whether the switch should show on the live site, and keeps only whether the switch is active.
  • To show the switch, the app adds its script to your site's pages with your settings (Manage Embedded Scripts permission). It does not read or change your products, prices, cart, checkout or orders.
  • If you ask Aria, the Wix AI assistant, about the app, the app returns your settings of the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account; we have no servers of our own. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Your settings stay on the site until you change them in the app dashboard. Removing the app takes the switch off your pages; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
