Related Products Picker privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Related Products Picker ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix stores that shows related products at the bottom of product pages. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from shoppers

  • When a shopper opens a product page, the shopper's browser asks the app for the related products of that product, sending only the product ID. The app answers with the name, photo, page address, price and stock status of the products to show.
  • The app does not store anything about shoppers: no names, emails, IP addresses, browsing history or clicks. It does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from store owners

  • The app stores, in data collections on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours: for each product you set up, its name and the IDs and names of the related products you chose, in your order; and the block settings (heading, number of products, same-category fill, hiding out-of-stock products and the products never to show). Only the app's server can read these.
  • The app reads your store's visible products from Wix Stores (Read Products permission) to show them in the block and in the product search in the dashboard. It does not change your products.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know which options apply. It does not store the plan details.
  • If you ask Aria about related products, the app returns a summary and the product names of your choices on the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Your choices and settings stay on the site until you change or delete them in the app dashboard. Removing the app takes the block off the site; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collections under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
