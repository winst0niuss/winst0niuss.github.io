The service

The app checks the old URLs you give it on your site's published domains. For each URL it shows where the redirects lead, the final status and whether it reached the expected page, and it flags missing pages, wrong pages, redirects to the home page, chains, loops, temporary redirects and canonical or language mismatches. On the Pro plan it can create missing 301 redirects for old URLs that return a missing page and have a new URL in your list, on single-language sites with a connected domain.

Your URLs and your redirects

You are responsible for the URLs you check and for the redirects of your site. Results reflect your site at the time of the check; changes made after that are not included. Review the results before you act on them. The app skips redirects that would clash with your existing ones, but it cannot see changes made in Wix at the same moment. Redirects it creates are yours to edit or delete in your URL redirect manager.

The app has one paid plan, Pro, with a free trial. Without a plan the app checks the first 25 URLs of each check; Pro checks up to 2,000 URLs at once and creates missing 301 redirects.
