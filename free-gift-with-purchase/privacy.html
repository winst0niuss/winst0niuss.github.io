Free Gift with Purchase privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Free Gift with Purchase ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix stores that gives shoppers a free product when their cart reaches an amount set by the store owner, and shows their progress in the cart. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from shoppers

  • The cart block loads the gift offer from the app's server on Wix. The request carries no shopper data, and the app does not collect or store shoppers' names, emails, IP addresses or any other personal data.
  • In the shopper's browser, the block reads the current cart to show the progress, and adds or removes the gift line through the Wix cart. The cart stays in Wix; the app does not copy it to its server.
  • The shopper's browser keeps the offer for up to 5 minutes and remembers, for the browsing session, whether the app added the gift and whether the shopper removed it. This stays in the shopper's browser session storage and contains no personal data.
  • Checkout, payments and orders are handled by Wix; the app does not see or store buyers' details or payment information.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app reads your store products (Read Products permission) so you can choose the gift, and keeps the gift's product ID, name, image and price.
  • The app creates and updates one automatic discount in your store (Manage eCommerce permission) that makes the gift free when the cart reaches your amount.
  • The app stores your offer settings in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours: the gift, the amount, texts, colors and whether the gift is added automatically. Only the app's server can read this collection.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know whether the block should show on the live site, and keeps only whether the plan is active and when it was checked.
  • If you ask Aria about your gift offer, the app returns the offer of the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Settings stay on the site until you change them in the app dashboard. Removing the app takes the block off your cart; the automatic discount stays in your store's Automatic Discounts, where you can delete it, and Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
