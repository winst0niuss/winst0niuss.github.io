PDF Viewer: Flipbook & Scroll privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

PDF Viewer: Flipbook & Scroll ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that shows PDF documents on site pages as a flipbook or a scrolling viewer. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from visitors

  • A viewer block loads its settings from the app's server on Wix. The request carries only the ID of the flipbook chosen for the block. The app does not collect or store visitors' names, emails, IP addresses or any other personal data.
  • The PDF file loads from your site's media on the Wix media servers. A few rare PDFs (for example, with Asian fonts that are not built into the file) also need font data, which the visitor's browser loads from the public jsDelivr CDN; no visitor data is sent with these requests.
  • To load faster, the visitor's browser keeps the shown flipbook settings in its own session storage for up to 5 minutes. This stays in the visitor's browser and contains only the file address and settings shown on the page.
  • Turning pages, scrolling and full screen happen in the visitor's browser and are not sent anywhere. The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your flipbooks — names, addresses, file names, sizes and page counts of the PDFs you choose from your site media, and look settings — in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours. Only the app's server can read this collection. The PDF files themselves stay in your site media.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know which flipbooks can show on the live site, and keeps only whether the Pro plan is active and when it was checked.
  • If you ask Aria about your flipbooks, the app returns their names, file names, page counts and settings of the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Your flipbooks stay on the site until you change or delete them in the app dashboard. Removing the app takes the viewer blocks off your pages; your PDF files stay in your site media, and Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
