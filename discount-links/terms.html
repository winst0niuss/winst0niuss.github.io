The service

The app turns a coupon of your store into a link to a page of your site. A shopper who opens the link gets the coupon applied to their cart once it has items, and can see a banner with the discount. The dashboard lets you create links, copy them, download QR codes and see how many times each link was opened and how many times the coupon was used.

Your coupons and your offer

You create and manage coupons in your store, decide their discount, limits and expiry, and are responsible for honoring them. The app applies a coupon only through Wix, and Wix decides whether it is valid at checkout. A cart can hold one coupon: if a shopper already entered another one, the app does not replace it. A shopper who uses "Buy Now" goes straight to checkout and may need to enter the code there. Share links only where you are allowed to offer the discount.

Plans and price

The app has one paid plan, Pro, with a free trial. You can set up links without a plan; links apply coupons on your live site while your trial or plan is active. Plans, prices, trials, billing, renewals and refunds are handled by Wix under its terms, as shown when you upgrade.
