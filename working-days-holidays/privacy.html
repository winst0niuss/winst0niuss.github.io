Working Days & Holidays privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Working Days & Holidays ("the app") is an app for monday.com that adds automation actions and a monday sidekick skill which calculate dates in working days. The app is developed and operated by Vadym Chumachenko, an individual developer ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Board data

  • When one of your automations runs, monday.com sends the app the item, the board and the columns you chose in that automation. With a short-lived token from monday.com, the app reads only the columns you chose in that automation, calculates the result and writes it to the column you chose.
  • Board data is processed in memory for that one run and is not stored by the app. The app does not read item names, people, updates, files or any other columns.

Sidekick requests

When you ask monday sidekick about working days, sidekick sends the app only the dates, the number of days and the country from your request. The app calculates the answer with your account settings and returns it to sidekick. These values are processed in memory and are not stored. The app does not receive your conversation and does not read or change boards for sidekick.

Account settings

  • Your work week, country, state or region and company days off are saved in monday.com app storage for your account, not in a database of ours. Admins of your account can change them on the app's settings page.
  • The settings page shows the next holidays and a calculator. It sends your country, region and draft settings to the app's server only to return those results; nothing is stored.
  • The app reads your app subscription from monday.com to know whether automations may run, and keeps it in memory for up to five minutes.

Logs

For each automation run and sidekick request the server logs the action name, your monday.com account ID and whether the date changed, and error messages if a run fails. Logs are written with the monday code logger, kept by monday.com's hosting under its retention rules and used only to keep the app working.

What we don't do

The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics, does not send emails or notifications, and does not share data with third parties. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on monday code, monday.com's hosting (EU region), and calls only the monday.com API. monday.com acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Settings stay in monday.com storage until you change them or uninstall the app; after uninstall monday.com handles app data under its own policies. To ask a question about your data or to have your settings deleted, email us.
