Last updated: November 12, 2026

These terms apply to your use of the Double Booking Rules & Limits app ("the app") on your Wix site. By installing the app you accept them. Your use of Wix and of Wix Bookings is covered by the terms of Wix.

The service

The app checks new and rescheduled bookings of Wix Bookings against rules you set in the app dashboard: services that share a space can't be booked at overlapping times (with optional cleaning time), a daily limit of bookings or participants, and one staff member for all services of a multi-service booking. Wix asks the app before it creates a booking; if a rule is broken, the booking is not created and Wix shows the customer its own message. The dashboard shows a log of stopped bookings.

Your rules and bookings

You decide which rules apply and to which services, and you are responsible for your services, prices, refunds and what you tell your customers. Courses are not checked. Bookings you or your staff add in the dashboard skip the rules unless you turn that on. If the app can't check a booking in time or has a problem, the booking goes through as if the app were not installed, so the app may not stop every booking that breaks a rule.
