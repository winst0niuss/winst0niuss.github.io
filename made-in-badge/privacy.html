Made In: Country of Origin privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Made In: Country of Origin ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix stores that shows where a product is made, with a country flag, on the product page. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from shoppers

  • On a product page, the shopper's browser asks the app's server on Wix for the badges of that product. The request carries only the product ID; the app does not collect or store shoppers' names, emails, IP addresses or any other personal data.
  • The browser keeps the product's badges for a few minutes in session storage so the next visit to the same product does not ask again. This contains no personal data.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics, and it does not see orders or payments.

Data from site owners

  • The app reads your store products (Read Products permissions) so you can find products by name and give them their own badges, and keeps the IDs of those products.
  • The app stores your settings in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours: the store-wide badge, the badges of chosen products, the look of the badge, and whether the app plan is active and when it was checked. Only the app's server can read this collection.
  • The app reads basic site information from Wix (site language and the app plan) to suggest a country for the first badge and to know whether badges should show on the live site.
  • If you ask Aria about your badges, the app returns the badges and the product list of the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Settings stay on the site until you change them in the app dashboard. Once the app is removed, badges stop showing. Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
