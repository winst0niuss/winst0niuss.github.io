Dimensional Shipping Rates privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Dimensional Shipping Rates ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix stores that calculates shipping rates at checkout from the site owner's boxes and dimensional weight. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from shoppers

  • When a shopper reaches checkout, Wix sends the app the cart: the products, their quantities, weights, sizes and prices, the store currency and the shipping address. The app uses only the products, quantities, weights, sizes, prices and currency of the cart and the country and state or region of the address, only to calculate the shipping rates it returns to Wix.
  • The app does not store carts or addresses, does not use any other shopper details, and does not add anything to your site pages.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your settings in data collections on your own Wix site: units, currency, divisor, weight step, handling fee, your boxes and your shipping options with their price tables, markup, free shipping amounts and countries.
  • Product sizes you enter on the Product sizes tab or import from a CSV file are stored in a data collection on your site, one record for each product: length, width, height, weight and the Own box mark.
  • The app also stores a record of your site's app plan, so checkout can check it quickly.
  • In your dashboard, the app reads the names, IDs and SKUs of your store products to show the Product sizes table. A CSV file you import is read in your browser; only the sizes in it are saved. An exported CSV file is built in your browser and saved to your computer.
  • At checkout, the app reads your settings, the product sizes of the products in the cart and your shipping delivery profiles from Wix, only to calculate rates and to return your backup rate when a cart can't be priced.
  • If you ask Aria about your shipping setup, the app's server reads your settings, the plan and the number of products with sizes and returns them to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.
  • Error logs on Wix hosting keep the reason a rate could not be calculated and the error message. The app does not log carts or addresses.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and its data is stored in your site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Settings and product sizes stay in your site's data collections until you change them. If you remove the app, Wix keeps them with your site, so they are back if you install the app again. Test a cart results are not saved. To ask a question about your data, email us.
