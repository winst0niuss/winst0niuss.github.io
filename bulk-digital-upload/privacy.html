Bulk Digital Product Upload privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Bulk Digital Product Upload ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix stores that turns files chosen by the site owner into digital products. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from shoppers

  • The app has no part on your live site and does not collect any data from site visitors or buyers. Buyers get their files from Wix Stores after paying.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • Files you choose are uploaded from your browser straight to your site's Media Manager: files for download as private files, product images as regular images. They do not pass through our servers.
  • The app creates products in your Wix Stores catalog with the names, prices, description, category and visibility you set, and can create a category you name.
  • The app keeps a history of your uploads (product IDs, product names, how many products were created, the category name and when products were deleted) in a data collection on the site's own Wix account. Only the app's server can read it.
  • The app reads the app plan, the site currency, the store catalog version and your store categories from Wix.
  • If you ask Aria about your uploads, the app returns the plan, the number of products created and recent uploads to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Products, files and the upload history stay on the site under the site owner's control. You can delete the products of an upload from the app, and delete files in the Media Manager. Removing the app does not delete your products or files; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
