Last updated: November 13, 2026

These terms apply to your use of the VAT Toggle: Prices Excl VAT app ("the app") on your Wix site. By installing the app you accept them. Your use of Wix itself is covered by the terms of Wix.

The service

The app adds a switch to your store so visitors can see Wix Stores prices including or excluding VAT. It recalculates the prices shown on store pages with the single VAT rate you set and can add a label next to them. The app changes only how prices are shown in the visitor's browser: it does not change your products, prices, tax settings, cart, checkout or orders, and visitors always pay your store price with tax as set up in your store.

Your settings

You are responsible for the VAT rate you set, for telling the app whether your product prices are entered with or without VAT, for your own labels, and for checking the prices on your site after changes. The app uses one rate for all products, so prices shown excluding VAT may differ from the tax charged at checkout if your products have different rates. Showing prices excluding VAT does not replace any price display your local law requires.

Plans and price

The app has one paid plan with a free trial. The switch shows on your live site only while your trial or plan is active; you can set up everything before that. Plans, prices, trials, billing, renewals and refunds are handled by Wix under its terms, as shown when you upgrade.
