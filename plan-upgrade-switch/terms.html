The service

The app adds a Change Plan block to your site. A logged-in member sees their current plan from the group of plans you choose and can switch to another plan in that group. If you turn credit on, the unused part of the member's paid period is counted as credit and given as a one-time coupon code for the first payment of the new plan. When the member buys a plan from the group, the app ends their earlier plan in the group: right away if credit was used, otherwise by the rule you choose. The dashboard shows a log of recent switches.

Your plans, prices and members

You decide which plans members can switch between, whether credit is given and when old plans end, and you are responsible for your prices, taxes, refunds and the terms you agree with your members. The credit is calculated by the formula shown in the app and is rounded down to the cent. Coupons are created in your site's Wix account; the member pays in the Wix Pricing Plans checkout. If Wix does not let the app end an old plan, the log shows it and you end that plan in Pricing Plans yourself.

Plans and price

The app has one paid plan with a free trial. The block shows on your live site, and old plans end automatically, only while your trial or plan is active; you can set up everything before that.
