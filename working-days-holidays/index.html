Working Days & Holidays for monday.com

Set due dates and timelines in working days. Your monday.com automations skip weekends, official public holidays of more than 200 countries, states and regions, and your company days off — for any work week. You can also ask monday sidekick: “What date is 10 working days after December 18?” or “Is December 26 a working day?”
