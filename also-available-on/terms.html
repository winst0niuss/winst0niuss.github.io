The service

The app shows an "Also available on:" bar on your product pages with buttons that link to the same product on other marketplaces or stores. You add these links in the app dashboard. Links open in a new tab. The app does not sell products, does not take payments, does not change your products, prices or checkout, and does not connect to your accounts on any marketplace.

Your links

You are responsible for keeping links correct and current, for having the right to sell the product where you link to, and for following the rules of each marketplace, including its rules on affiliate links, affiliate disclosures and the use of its name. When a product has links for several countries, the first country shown is suggested from the shopper's browser language; the app does not detect where the shopper is, and shoppers can choose another country. We do not promise that every theme or page layout shows the bar; check your product pages after setup.
