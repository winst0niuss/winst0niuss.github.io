Social Media Card privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Social Media Card ("the app", "we") is a free app for Wix sites that shows a card with links to the site owner's social media profiles. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from site visitors

The app does not collect or store personal data about visitors. To show the card, it reads the card settings from the site's Wix account on the server and sends only what the card shows to the visitor's browser: the title, the network names, the profile links, the numbers and the look settings. When a visitor clicks a link, the social network's own page opens under that network's privacy policy.

Data from site owners

  • Card settings (title, networks, profile links, names, follower numbers and look) are stored in a data collection on the site's own Wix account, not on servers of ours.
  • The app does not connect to social networks, does not log in to them and does not read data from them.
  • If you ask Aria about your card, the app returns the card details of the current site to Aria inside Wix.

We do not use cookies or browser storage, we do not track visitors, we do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Settings stay on the site until the site owner changes them in the app. Removing the app takes the card off the site. Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
