Gift Wrap: Gift Message privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Gift Wrap: Gift Message ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix stores that lets shoppers add gift wrapping and a gift message to their order in the cart. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from shoppers

  • The cart block loads the gift wrap offer from the app's server on Wix. This request carries no shopper data.
  • When a shopper adds or edits gift wrap, the block sends the app's server the cart ID, the chosen wrapping style and the gift note the shopper typed (To, From and the message). The server checks that the cart belongs to this shopper and adds the gift wrap line with the note to the Wix cart. The app does not keep the note: it stays in the Wix cart and, after purchase, in the store's order.
  • In the shopper's browser, the block reads the current cart to show the gift wrap, and removes or corrects the gift wrap line through the Wix cart. The cart stays in Wix; the app does not copy it to its server.
  • The shopper's browser keeps the offer for up to 5 minutes in session storage. It contains no personal data.
  • Checkout, payments and orders are handled by Wix; the app does not see or store buyers' details or payment information.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your gift wrap settings in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours: wrapping styles with their names, prices and photos, whether the gift message field is on, texts and the accent color. Only the app's server can read this collection.
  • The app adds gift wrap lines to your store's carts (Manage eCommerce – Admin, Read Carts and Write Carts permissions), with the name and price from your settings.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know whether the block should show on the live site, and keeps only whether the plan is active and when it was checked.
  • If you ask Aria about your gift wrap setup, the app returns the setup of the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Settings stay on the site until you change them in the app dashboard. Gift notes are part of the carts and orders in your store and are kept by Wix with them. Removing the app takes the block off your cart, and Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
