CMS Import: CSV Preview privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

CMS Import: CSV Preview ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that compares a CSV file chosen by the site owner with a CMS collection before the file is imported. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from site visitors

  • The app has no part on your live site and does not collect any data from site visitors.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The CSV file you choose is read in your browser and is not uploaded to our servers or stored anywhere.
  • The app reads the list of your CMS collections, their fields and their items from Wix in your browser, with the permissions of your Wix role, to compare them with the file. It never changes, adds or deletes items.
  • The safe import file is built in your browser and saved to your computer. It is not sent anywhere.
  • The app's server reads the app plan of your site from Wix. It does not read your collection items.
  • If you ask Aria about checking a CSV, the app's server reads your collection list and item counts from Wix and returns your collection names, the number of items in each, whether a safe import file is available and the plan to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and your data stays in your browser and in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

The app keeps no copy of your files or collections. Results are lost when you close or reload the page. Removing the app does not change your collections. To ask a question about your data, email us.
