The service

The app gives your customers a personal referral code and link after checkout, and to any visitor by email from the "Refer & earn" button. A friend who opens the link gets your friend discount applied to the cart; the code also works when typed at checkout. When a friend pays for an order with the code, the customer who shared it gets a one-time reward coupon by email. The dashboard shows customers in the program, friend orders, sales with referral codes and rewards, and lets you block a code.

Your program and your coupons

You decide the friend discount, the reward, its expiry and the monthly reward limit, and you are responsible for honoring them and for the rules of your program. Codes and rewards are regular Wix coupons, and Wix decides whether a coupon is valid at checkout. The app gives no reward for a customer's own code, for a friend who already bought with a code or above your monthly limit, and turns an unused reward off when the friend's order is canceled or fully refunded. A reward that was already used cannot be taken back. Make sure your program complies with the laws and platform rules that apply to you.

Emails

The app sends transactional emails on your behalf through Wix: personal links and reward coupons. Wix delivers them under its terms.
