The service

The app shows a cookie consent banner on every page of your site and records each visitor's choice in your site's privacy settings, which decide which analytics, advertising and other tools may run for that visitor. When you turn the banner on, the app sets your site's default consent to essential cookies only; when you turn it off or remove the app, the default goes back to allowing all cookies.

The app is a tool and not legal advice. You decide whether and how your site must ask for consent, write your cookie or privacy policy, choose the categories and texts the banner shows, and check that the tools on your site follow visitors' choices. Tools added outside the site's privacy settings, for example code that ignores them, may not be blocked by the banner.
