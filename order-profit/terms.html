The service

The app shows the net profit and margin of the paid orders of your Wix store in the period you pick: what the customer paid without tax and refunds, minus product cost, shipping cost, payment fees and packaging. It also shows profit by promotion, lists products without a cost and exports the order table to CSV. The app reads your orders and products and never changes them. A period can include up to 2,000 orders.

Your numbers

Profit is an estimate made from the data you provide: product costs in Wix Stores or in the app, the payment fees and packaging cost in Settings, and the carrier files you upload. Without a carrier file, shipping cost is what the customer paid for shipping, or zero. Profit uses the current product cost, not the cost at the time of the order, and chargebacks are not counted. You are responsible for checking these numbers. The app is not accounting or tax advice.

Plans and price
