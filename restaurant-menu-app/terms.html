The service

The app turns the menus you keep in Wix Restaurants Menus into a mobile menu that guests open by a link or QR code. In the app dashboard you choose which menus guests see and when, mark the allergens of each dish, pick the look and the guest languages, and translate your dish texts. The guest menu is shown on our page at a permanent address for your restaurant. The app does not take orders or payments and does not change your menus, dishes or prices.

Your content and allergen information

Dishes, prices, photos and sold-out marks come from your restaurant menus. Allergen information and translations are entered by you, or translated by machine when you use auto-translate. You are responsible for their accuracy. The app shows guests a note to confirm allergens with staff and marks dishes without allergen information, but it does not check your information and is not a substitute for asking staff. Machine translations can be wrong; check them before you save.

Saved copies on guests' phones

To open quickly and without a connection, a guest's phone keeps a copy of the menu after a visit. When it shows that copy offline, the menu says that prices, availability and allergens may have changed.
