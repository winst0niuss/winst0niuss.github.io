Accessibility Widget: WCAG EAA privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Accessibility Widget: WCAG EAA ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that lets visitors adjust a site for themselves: text size, spacing, contrast, highlighted links and less motion. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from visitors

  • The widget gets its look and list of tools from the page itself. It makes no requests to the app's server and does not collect or store visitors' names, emails, IP addresses or any other personal data.
  • The tools a visitor turns on are saved in that visitor's browser (local storage), only when the visitor has allowed functional cookies in the site's privacy settings, so the same settings apply on the next pages. If the visitor withdraws that consent, the saved settings are deleted. They never leave the browser.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your widget settings — position, color, size, tools and your statement link — in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours. Only the app's server can read this collection.
  • With the Pro plan, the app asks Wix to scan your published pages for accessibility issues and shows you the results. The scan and its results are run and kept by Wix (for about 6 months) under the terms of Wix; the app does not copy them elsewhere.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know whether Pro is active.
  • If you ask Aria about your widget, the app returns its settings for the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Keeping and deleting data

Your settings stay on the site until you change them in the app dashboard. Removing the app in your site's Manage Apps takes the widget off your pages; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
