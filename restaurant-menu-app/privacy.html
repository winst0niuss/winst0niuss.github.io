QR Menu & Allergens privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

QR Menu & Allergens ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix restaurants that shows their menus to guests as a mobile menu opened by a QR code. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from guests

  • When a guest opens a restaurant's menu, our page reads that restaurant's published menu from its Wix site and shows it. The app does not ask guests for any personal data and does not store anything about them on our side: no names, emails, IP addresses, orders or browsing history.
  • The guest's own phone keeps a copy of the menu so it opens without a connection, and remembers the guest's choices of language and light or dark mode and whether the guest declined saving the menu to the phone. These stay on the phone and are not sent to us.
  • The app uses no cookies, tracking or analytics of its own. Our page runs on Wix hosting, which may load its own scripts under the Wix privacy policy.

Data from restaurant owners

  • The app stores, in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours: your menu settings (which menus guests see, their hours, time zone, theme, colors, cover photo and icon links, app name, guest languages and order link), the allergens you mark for each dish, your dish translations, and a copy of the app plan, currency, language, site name and address so the guest menu opens quickly. Only the app can read these.
  • The app reads your menus, sections, dishes, prices, sizes and labels from Wix Restaurants Menus to show them to guests and in the dashboard. Wix offers this access only as a full Restaurants permission; the app only reads and does not change your menus.
  • When you press auto-translate, the app sends the texts you chose to the Wix Multilingual machine translation service and shows you the result. Wix deducts the words from your site's translation credits. Nothing is saved until you save it.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know whether guests can see the menu.
  • If you ask Aria about the app, it returns a summary of your menu app (menus, hours, dishes without allergen info) to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app and the guest page run on Wix hosting, and your data stays in the site's Wix account. To show the menu, our page keeps it in memory for about a minute. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Your settings, allergens and translations stay on the site until you change or delete them in the app dashboard. Removing the app stops the guest menu at your link; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. Guests can clear the saved copy by removing the menu from their phone or clearing the browser's site data. To ask a question about your data, email us.
