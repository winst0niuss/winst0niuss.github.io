The service

The app estimates staff pay from your Wix Bookings data and the pay rules you set, and shows how each amount was calculated.

An estimate, not payroll

Results are estimates to help you. The app is not a payroll, tax or accounting service: it does not calculate taxes or deductions, send payments or give legal or financial advice. Results depend on your rules and on the data in Wix Bookings, such as prices, attendance and staff assignments. A booking without an order uses the service price, and a partial refund isn't deducted; the report marks these. A full refund removes commission only, unpaid bookings are counted, and amounts are added up without currency conversion. You are responsible for checking every amount before paying anyone, and for complying with employment and tax law.
