Tiered Discounts: Progress Bar privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Tiered Discounts: Progress Bar ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix stores that gives shoppers a bigger discount for bigger carts and shows their progress to the next tier. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from shoppers

  • The window gets the tiers and its look from the page itself and reads the current cart in the shopper's browser to show the progress. The cart stays in Wix; the app does not copy it to its server.
  • When a shopper clicks Checkout Bundle, the browser asks the app's server on Wix for the checkout link of the shopper's current cart. The server uses the cart only to get that link and stores nothing. The cart page block loads the tiers from the app's server; that request carries no shopper data.
  • The app does not collect or store shoppers' names, emails, IP addresses or any other personal data. The shopper's browser remembers, for the browsing session, whether the window is folded; this stays in session storage and contains no personal data.
  • Checkout, payments and orders are handled by Wix; the app does not see or store buyers' details or payment information.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app reads your store products (Read Products permission) so you can choose the discounted products, and keeps their product IDs and names.
  • The app creates, updates and deletes one automatic discount per tier in your store (Manage eCommerce permission).
  • The app adds its window script to your site pages, with your tiers and look as its settings.
  • The app stores your settings in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours: tiers, products, texts, colors and window position. Only the app's server can read this collection.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know whether the discounts and the window should work on the live site, and keeps only whether the plan is active and when it was checked.
  • If you ask Aria about your tiers, the app returns the tiers of the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Settings stay on the site until you change them in the app dashboard. Once the app is removed, the tier discounts stop applying, because each of them depends on the app; switch the discounts off and save before removing the app to also delete them from your store's Automatic Discounts. Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
