Preorder Delays: Ship Dates privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Preorder Delays: Ship Dates ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix stores that lines up pre-orders against incoming batches, shows a ship date on the product page and emails buyers their date. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. The store owner decides which buyers are emailed and is responsible for the buyers' data in their store.

Data from shoppers and buyers

  • On the product page, the app requests the ship date of that product from its server. The request contains only the product ID, and the app does not store anything about visitors. The answer is kept in the browser's session storage for 5 minutes, so the date is not requested again on each visit to the page. The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.
  • The app reads the store's approved orders from Wix and never changes them. For the queue it uses the order number and date, the buyer's name, email address and language, the ordered products with their options and quantities, refunds and shipped quantities. Orders placed before the pre-order start date of a product are not counted.
  • When Wix approves a new order with a product on the board, the app stores the order ID, the product ID, the time and the number of units, so the order is counted once.
  • Buyer emails are sent through Wix to the buyer's email address from the order, in English or Spanish by the language of the order. The name and address are read from the order when the email is sent. After an email is sent, the app stores which order line it was for, the order number, the email address it was sent to, the ship date in it and the time it was sent, so the queue shows who has been told. Buyer names are not stored. The app does not send marketing emails.

Data from store owners

  • The app stores, in private data collections on your own Wix site: the products on your board with their name, batches (arrival date, quantity and note) and pre-order start date; the number of units ordered, the IDs of orders already counted, when the queue was last checked and when you were last alerted; your settings (email templates, sender name, reply-to address and on/off switches for the product page date, automatic emails and alerts); and a record of your site's app plan, so the product page can check it quickly.
  • In your dashboard, the app reads the names, IDs and SKUs of your store products to let you add them to the board, and it reads the orders of board products in your browser to build the queue.
  • The app reads the site owner's email address and the app plan from Wix. The email address is used as the default reply-to address of buyer emails and to send you shortage alerts. It is not stored.
  • When pre-orders exceed the units on the way, the app sends you an email through Wix and a Wix dashboard notification with the product name and the numbers of ordered and incoming units.
  • The CSV file of the queue is built in your browser and saved to your computer. It includes buyer names and email addresses. It is not sent anywhere.
  • If you ask Aria about your pre-orders, the app's server returns your board to Aria inside Wix: product names, batches, pre-order start dates, ordered and incoming units, the next ship date and the plan status. Buyer names and email addresses are not included. Aria can only read, not change anything.
  • The app does not write buyer names or email addresses to its error logs.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and its data stays in your browser and in the site's Wix account. Emails and notifications are sent through Wix, and emails use your site's email quota in Wix. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Board data and settings stay in your site's data collections until you change them. When you remove a product from the board, its batches, start date and unit count are deleted; stored order records and records of sent emails are kept. The app has no automatic deletion period. To ask a question about your data, email us.
