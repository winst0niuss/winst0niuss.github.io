The service

The app shows a block of related products at the bottom of your Wix Stores product pages. In the app dashboard you choose related products for each product and their order; free spots in the block are filled with visible products from the same category. The block links to your own product pages. The app does not sell products, does not take payments and does not change your products, prices, stock or checkout.

Your choices

You decide which products are shown as related and in what order. Product names, photos, prices, sale prices and stock come from your store and are shown as your store provides them. Products hidden in your store are never shown. Products filled from the same category are chosen by the app's own rules, without sorting by sales or popularity. We do not promise that every theme or page layout shows the block; check your product pages after setup.

Plans and price

The app has a free plan with hand-picked related products for a limited number of products and same-category suggestions on every product page, and a paid plan without that limit and with options to hide out-of-stock products and keep chosen products out of the block. Plans, prices, trials, billing, renewals and refunds are handled by Wix under its terms, as shown when you upgrade. If you move from the paid plan to the free plan, your choices are kept, but the paid options and picks beyond the free limit stop applying.
