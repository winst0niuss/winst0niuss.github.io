Shabbat & Holiday Closing privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Shabbat & Holiday Closing ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix stores that closes checkout for Shabbat and Jewish holidays and shows shoppers when ordering reopens. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from shoppers

  • The notice on your pages is calculated in the shopper's browser from your settings, which are part of the page. The notice sends nothing to us or to the app's server.
  • If a shopper closes the full-screen message, the browser remembers it for that closing in its own session storage, so the message doesn't come back on every page. This stays in the shopper's browser.
  • At the cart and checkout, Wix asks the app whether orders are allowed right now (Wix Validations permission). The app decides only by the current time and your settings. It does not read or store the shopper's name, address, email, cart contents or payment details, apart from the shopper's language, used to show the message in English or Hebrew.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from store owners

  • The app stores your settings in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours: the place you chose (name, coordinates and time zone), candle lighting and Havdalah settings, the holidays you keep, your own closings, a manual close or reopen, and the look and notes of the notice. Only the app's server can read this collection.
  • So that the notice needs no requests, the app also puts these settings into the code it adds to your site's pages (Manage Embedded Scripts permission), where anyone viewing the page can see them. They contain no personal data.
  • The city list is built into the app; the city you search for is not sent anywhere.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know whether the notice and checkout blocking should work. It does not store the plan details.
  • If you ask Aria whether the store is closed, the app returns the current state, the next closings and your time settings of the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Settings stay on the site until you change them in the app dashboard. Removing the app takes the notice off your pages and stops checkout blocking; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
