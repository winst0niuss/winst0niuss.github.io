Event Submissions & Approval privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Event Submissions & Approval ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that lets visitors submit events through a form, checks each submission with AI and lets the site owner publish good events to the site's Wix Events calendar. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from visitors who submit an event

  • The organizer's name and email address and the event details they type: event name, start and end time, venue name and address or that the event is online, a description, an optional link and, if they add one, an image. The time zone of the visitor's browser is saved with the times. Before upload the image is made smaller in the visitor's browser, which also removes metadata such as the location where it was taken.
  • An anonymous visitor id that Wix gives to every site visitor (or the member id, if the visitor is logged in as a site member), the language of the page and the time of sending. The app uses them to limit how often one visitor can send events and to write the AI note and emails in the right language. The app does not ask for or store phone numbers or IP addresses.
  • The organizer name and the event details are sent for an AI review through the AI service built into the Wix platform (Wix AI APIs), together with the calendar topic the site owner set. The review is paid for with the site's AI credits; the email address and image are not sent. The AI returns a verdict, a list of issues and a short note for the site owner.
  • Submissions, their AI review and their status are stored in data collections of the app on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours. Images are stored in an Event Submissions folder of the site's Media Manager. Only the site owner and the site's collaborators see submissions in the dashboard; visitors see an event only after the site owner publishes it, with the event details, the image, the link and the line "Submitted by" with the organizer's name. The email address is never published.
  • Submissions that look automated, such as a filled hidden field or a form sent within seconds, are discarded and not stored.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your settings in the same site's data collection: the AI check and calendar topic, automatic publishing, form options, online events, images, RSVP, blocked words, look options, notification choices and the language of your dashboard for emails.
  • To publish an event, the app creates an event or a draft in your site's Wix Events (Manage Events permission) and keeps its id to publish a draft later.
  • To review submissions with AI, the app calls the AI service of the Wix platform on behalf of your site (Invoke AI Models permission).
  • To tell you about new submissions, the app reads the site owner's email (Read Site Owner Email permission) and sends an email through Wix (Manage Email Marketing permission), plus a notification in your dashboard and the Wix app (Manage Notifications permission). The email does not include the organizer's name or the event details. You can turn each off in the settings.
  • Images are uploaded to and removed from your Media Manager (Manage Media Manager permission).
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know whether the form should show on the live site, and keeps only whether the plan is active and when it was checked.
  • If you ask Aria about your event submissions, the app returns counts of submissions and your settings to Aria inside Wix, never names, emails or event texts. Aria can only read, not change anything.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising. As the site owner, you decide what to publish and are responsible for telling your visitors how you use their events and contact details.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. The AI review runs through the Wix platform's AI service, which may use AI model providers engaged by Wix. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Submissions stay in the app until the site owner deletes them in the app dashboard; deleting a submission also removes its image, unless the event is already in Wix Events. Events published or saved as drafts live in the site's Wix Events and are managed there. An organizer who wants an event or their details removed can ask the site owner. When the site owner uninstalls the app, Wix may keep or delete the app's data collections under its own rules; events already in Wix Events and images in the Media Manager stay until the site owner deletes them. To ask a question about your data, email us.
