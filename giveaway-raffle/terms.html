The service

The app shows a giveaway on your site: the prize, a countdown and an entry form with a box to accept your official rules. It keeps the entries on your site, adds entrants to your Wix contacts if you turn this on, and lets you draw a random winner, draw again, and export the entries as CSV. It can email you and notify you in your dashboard when the giveaway ends.
