Discount Links: Apply Coupons privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Discount Links: Apply Coupons ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix stores that turns a store coupon into a link: a shopper who opens the link gets the coupon applied to their cart. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from shoppers

  • When a shopper opens a link with a coupon code, the shopper's browser remembers the code, a short description of the discount and whether it was applied, for up to seven days. This note is kept in the browser's local storage, holds no personal data and is removed after an order or when the coupon stops working.
  • The app adds the coupon to the shopper's current cart through Wix. It does not read or store names, addresses, emails or payment details.
  • The app counts how many times each link was opened. Only the count is stored, on the site's own Wix account; no IP address, device or visitor identifier is kept with it.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your links (coupon code, page and your label), the banner settings and the click counts in a data collection on the site's own Wix account. Only the app's server can read it.
  • The app reads your store's coupons from Wix to let you choose one, to describe the discount on the banner and to show how many times each coupon was used. It does not create, change or delete coupons.
  • The app reads the app plan, the site address and the site currency from Wix to build links and show amounts.
  • If you ask Aria about your links, the app returns the links, their click counts and coupon uses to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Links and counts stay on the site under the site owner's control; deleting a link removes it from the app. Removing the app takes the script off your pages; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collections under its own rules. Coupons stay in your store. Shoppers can clear the stored code by clearing their browser's site data. To ask a question about your data, email us.
