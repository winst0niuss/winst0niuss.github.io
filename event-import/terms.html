The service

The app imports events from a CSV file you choose into your site's events. Before anything is written, it shows which rows are new, changed, unchanged or have errors, and the old and new value of each changed field. When you click Import, it creates and updates events one by one, and you can stop at any time. Rows are matched to events by Event ID, by your own key column or by title, so the same file can be uploaded again to update the same events. The app can also export your events to a CSV file. It never deletes or cancels events.

Your files and your data

You are responsible for the files you import and for the content of your events. Review the preview before you import. The preview reflects your events at the time of the check; if an event is changed elsewhere after the check, the app skips that row instead of overwriting it. Import works with the permissions of your Wix role. The app imports files of up to 1,000 rows and 5 MB.
