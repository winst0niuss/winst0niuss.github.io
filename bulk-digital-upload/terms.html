The service

The app turns files you choose into digital products in your Wix Stores catalog. It uploads each file to your site's Media Manager as a private file, creates a product with that file as its download, and can add a product image, a price, a description, a category and a watermark on photo previews. It keeps a history of your uploads so you can delete the products of one upload. Selling, checkout and delivering files to buyers are done by Wix Stores.

Your files and your products

You upload only files you have the right to sell, and you are responsible for your products, prices, descriptions and what buyers receive. Check the names, prices and settings in the list before creating products. Deleting products from the history removes them from your store; their files stay in your Media Manager. The app works with the current Wix Stores catalog; on stores that still use the previous catalog it explains this and creates nothing.

Plans and price

The app has one paid plan, Pro, with a free trial. Without a plan you can create up to 10 products to try the app. Plans, prices, trials, billing, renewals and refunds are handled by Wix under its terms, as shown when you upgrade.
