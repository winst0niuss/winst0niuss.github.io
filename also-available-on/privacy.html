Also Available On privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Also Available On ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix stores that shows buttons to the same product on other marketplaces, like Amazon, eBay or Etsy. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from shoppers

The app does not collect or store personal data about shoppers. To show the bar, the shopper's browser reads the links of that product and the look settings from the site's data collection through Wix. When a product has links for several countries, the browser picks the first country from its own language settings on the page; this is not sent to us or stored. The app does not use cookies or browser storage, does not count clicks and does not contact any marketplace. When a shopper clicks a button, the marketplace's page opens in a new tab under that marketplace's own privacy policy.

Data from store owners

  • The app stores, in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours: for each product with links, the product ID and its links (marketplace, link address, the name of another store, country and button color), and the look settings (heading, button colors, style and alignment). This data is public, because the product pages show it.
  • Any affiliate or tracking tags in a link are part of the link the store owner enters; the app stores and shows the link as entered.
  • The app reads product names from the store catalog (Read Products) so the owner can choose products in the dashboard, and checks that a product belongs to the site before saving its links. It stores only the product ID.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to apply plan limits. It does not store it.
  • If you ask Aria about your links, the app returns how many products have links and the links of the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Links and settings stay on the site until the store owner changes or deletes them in the app dashboard. Links of a product that was deleted from the store stay until the owner deletes them in the dashboard. Removing the app takes the bar off the site; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
