Order Profit: Margin Tracker privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Order Profit: Margin Tracker ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix stores that shows the net profit and margin of each order in the site owner's dashboard. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from shoppers

  • The app adds nothing to your site pages and does not collect anything from your site visitors.
  • In your dashboard, the app reads your store orders to calculate their profit: order numbers, dates, currency, totals, tax, shipping, discounts and promotion names, refunds and the products and quantities in each order. It also reads the tracking numbers of your shipments to match a carrier file by tracking number. It does not use customer names, email addresses or postal addresses.
  • The calculation runs in your browser. The app does not copy your orders to its own storage.

Data from site owners

  • The app reads your store products and the cost of goods of their variants from Wix Stores, only to calculate profit. It does not change your orders or products.
  • The app stores in data collections on your own Wix site: your settings (payment fee, fixed fee, packaging cost, the shipping rule and the refund option), the product costs you add in the app, and the shipping costs from the carrier files you upload, one record for each order number with its amount. A carrier file is read in your browser; only the order numbers and amounts are saved.
  • After each calculation, the app saves a short summary of the period on your site for Aria: the period, the number of orders, revenue, net profit, margin, up to 10 order numbers with the biggest loss and up to 10 names of products without a cost. If you ask Aria about your profit, the app's server returns this summary and your plan to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.
  • An exported CSV file is built in your browser and saved to your computer.
  • Error logs on Wix hosting keep the reason a request failed and the error message.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and its data is stored in your site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Settings, product costs, shipping costs and the summary stay in your site's data collections until you change or remove them. You can clear the product costs and remove the uploaded shipping costs in the app at any time. If you remove the app, Wix keeps this data with your site, so it is back if you install the app again. To ask a question about your data, email us.
