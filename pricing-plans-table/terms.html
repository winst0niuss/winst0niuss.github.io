The service

The app shows the public plans of your site from Wix Pricing Plans as a pricing table: plan cards, a monthly/yearly switch with the saving, a Most popular badge, price on request with a button to your contact link and an optional comparison of benefits. Buttons open the Wix checkout for the plan. The app does not take payments and does not create, change or delete your plans, prices, members or orders.

Your plans and settings

Names, prices, periods, trials and benefits are shown as they are set in Wix Pricing Plans. Monthly and yearly plans are paired by name and the saving is calculated from their prices; you can change the pairs in the dashboard. You are responsible for your plans, prices, the contact link you set and for checking the table on your site after changes. Prices, taxes, discounts and coupons applied at checkout are decided by Wix and your Pricing Plans settings and may differ from what the table shows.
