The service

The app adds a guestbook widget to your site: visitors leave a name, a message and optionally an emoji and a photo; you approve, hide or delete entries in the app dashboard, export them to CSV and get an email and a notification about new entries. Entries with links, blocked words or photos always wait for your approval, and the app limits how often entries can be posted.

Your content and your visitors

Entries are written by your visitors. You decide what is published and are responsible for the entries you publish, for photos stored in your Media Manager and for informing your visitors about how their entries are used. The spam protection reduces, but cannot fully prevent, unwanted entries. Entries may not be kept after you uninstall the app: export them first if you want to keep them.
