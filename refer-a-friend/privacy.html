Refer a Friend: Referral Codes privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Refer a Friend: Referral Codes ("the app", "we") is a referral program for Wix stores: customers get a personal code and link, friends who use it get a discount, and the customer who sent them gets a reward coupon after the friend pays. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from customers who share a code

  • After checkout, the app reads that order through Wix to give the buyer a personal code. Wix returns the order only to the visitor who placed it. The app stores the buyer's email, first name and language, the code, and counts of friends, sales and rewards.
  • A visitor who asks for a link with the "Refer & earn" button gives an email and, optionally, a first name. The link is sent only by email. The app stores the time of the last email to limit repeats.
  • The app sends these customers transactional emails through Wix: their personal link and reward coupons.

Data from friends

  • When a friend opens a referral link, the friend's browser remembers the code and whether the discount was applied, for up to seven days. This note is kept in the browser's local storage, holds no personal data and is removed after an order.
  • When an order with a referral code is paid, approved or canceled, the app reads it through Wix and stores the order number, the buyer's email, the order total and currency, the code and the reward status. The email is used to check whether the friend bought with a code before and is shown only to the site owner. It is never shown or sent to the customer who shared the code.
  • The app does not read or store addresses or payment details, and does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores the program settings, the site address and currency, and a secret key used to create reward codes in data collections on the site's own Wix account. Only the app's server can read them.
  • The app creates, updates and turns off store coupons for referral codes and rewards. They are regular Wix coupons and stay in your store.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know whether the program is active.
  • If you ask Aria about your program, the app returns totals and settings to Aria inside Wix, without emails or names. Aria can only read, not change anything.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider and sends the emails. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Customers, orders and settings stay on the site under the site owner's control. Removing the app takes the script off your pages; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collections under its own rules. Coupons stay in your store. Friends can clear the stored code by clearing their browser's site data. Customers can ask the site owner to remove their data. To ask a question about your data, email us.
