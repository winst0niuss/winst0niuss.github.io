Before After: Image Comparison privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Before After: Image Comparison ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that shows two photos as a before and after slider on site pages. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from visitors

  • A slider block loads its photos, labels and look settings from the app's server on Wix. The request carries only the ID of the comparison chosen for the block. The app does not collect or store visitors' names, emails, IP addresses or any other personal data.
  • Photos load from your site's media on the Wix media servers, in a size that fits the visitor's screen.
  • To load faster, the visitor's browser keeps the shown comparison in its own session storage for up to 5 minutes. This stays in the visitor's browser and contains only the photo addresses and settings shown on the page.
  • Moving the slider happens in the visitor's browser and is not sent anywhere. The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your comparisons — names, addresses of the photos you choose from your site media, photo descriptions, labels and look settings — in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours. Only the app's server can read this collection. The photos themselves stay in your site media.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know whether comparisons should show on the live site, and keeps only whether the plan is active and when it was checked.
  • If you ask Aria about your comparisons, the app returns their names, labels and settings of the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Your comparisons stay on the site until you change or delete them in the app dashboard. Removing the app takes the slider blocks off your pages; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
