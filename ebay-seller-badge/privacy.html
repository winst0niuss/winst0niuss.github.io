Seller Badge for eBay privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Seller Badge for eBay ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that shows the site owner's eBay seller profile in a badge: name, positive feedback percentage, feedback score and a Shop on eBay button. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy. What eBay itself does with data is covered by the eBay User Privacy Notice.

Data from site visitors

The app does not collect or store personal data about visitors. To show the badge, the visitor's browser asks the app's server on Wix for the badge, and the server sends back only what the badge shows: the name, the profile link, the positive feedback percentage, the feedback score and the look settings. Visitors' browsers do not contact eBay until a visitor clicks the name or the Shop on eBay button; then eBay's own page opens under eBay's privacy notice.

Data from site owners

  • What you enter. In the app dashboard you enter your eBay username or a link to your eBay profile and, on the paid plan, your feedback score and positive feedback percentage copied from your eBay profile. The app does not ask you to sign in to eBay, never sees your eBay password and does not connect to eBay at all.
  • What the app stores, in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours: what you entered; the badge settings (eBay site for the link, the name to show, layout, colors, theme, size, which parts to show); the app plan and the app instance ID read from Wix, and when they were checked.
  • Aria. If you ask Aria about your badge, the app returns the badge details and update status of the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

We do not use cookies or browser storage on your visitors' devices, we do not track visitors, we do not sell data, we do not share it with third parties and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. The app does not send any data to eBay. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

  • What you entered stays until you change or clear it in the app dashboard.
  • Badge settings stay on the site until you change them. Removing the app takes the badge off the site; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules.

To ask a question about your data or ask us to delete it, email us.
