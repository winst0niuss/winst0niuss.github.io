The service

The app shows a delivery check on your product pages: shoppers enter a ZIP code or postcode and see whether you deliver there, with the delivery time, estimated cost and note you set for that delivery area. It lists the codes shoppers checked outside your areas. If you turn it on, it also checks the delivery address at checkout and warns shoppers or stops orders to addresses outside your areas. The app does not take payments, does not change your products, prices or shipping rates, and does not deliver anything.

Your delivery areas and answers

You set every delivery area, code, delivery time, cost and note, and you are responsible for keeping them correct and for delivering as promised. The delivery cost the app shows is an estimate you enter; shoppers pay what your shipping settings charge at checkout. When codes match several areas, the first area in your list wins.

Add regions and Add by distance fill areas from postal code data built into the app: U.S. Census Bureau data for the United States and GeoNames data (geonames.org, CC BY 4.0, modified) for nine European countries. This data may be incomplete or out of date: for example, P.O. box-only ZIP codes are not included, and distance is measured between postal code centers, not by road. Review the codes before you save them.

Checkout check

The checkout check is off by default. If you choose not to allow orders outside your areas, shoppers with an address outside them can't complete the order, so use it only if you deliver just to these areas. Store pickup and orders with only digital items are not checked. If the app can't read your settings at checkout, it lets the order through rather than block it. We do not promise that every checkout setup, delivery option or address format is recognized; test your checkout after setup.
