Pricing Plans Table privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Pricing Plans Table ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that shows the site's pricing plans as a pricing table with a monthly/yearly switch, a Most popular badge, price on request and a comparison of benefits. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from visitors

  • The table loads the site's public plans and your table settings from the app's server on Wix. The request carries no visitor data, and the app does not collect or store visitors' names, emails, IP addresses or any other personal data.
  • To load faster, the visitor's browser keeps the table in its own session storage for up to 5 minutes. This stays in the visitor's browser and contains only the plans and settings shown on the page.
  • When a visitor chooses a plan, the button opens the Wix checkout for that plan. Purchases, payments and member accounts are handled by Wix; the app does not see or store the buyer's details or payment information.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app reads the site's public plans from Wix Pricing Plans (Read Pricing Plans permission): names, descriptions, prices, billing periods, free trials, fees and benefits. Private plans are not shown. The amount of a plan with price on request is removed on the server and never reaches the visitor's browser.
  • The app stores your table settings in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours: plan order, hidden plans, the highlighted plan, monthly and yearly pairs, button and badge texts, the contact link, colors and layout. Only the app's server can read this collection.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know whether the table should show on the live site, and keeps only whether the plan is active and when it was checked.
  • If you ask Aria about your pricing table, the app returns the plans and settings of the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Settings stay on the site until you change them in the app dashboard. Removing the app takes the table off your pages; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. Your plans in Wix Pricing Plans are not changed by the app. To ask a question about your data, email us.
