Event Import: CSV Bulk Update privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Event Import: CSV Bulk Update ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that imports and updates the site's events from a CSV file chosen by the site owner. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from site visitors

  • The app has no part on your live site and does not collect any data from site visitors, guests or ticket buyers.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The CSV file you choose is read in your browser and is not uploaded to our servers or stored anywhere.
  • The app's dashboard page reads your site's events from Wix in your browser, with the permissions of your Wix role, to compare them with the file. When you click Import, it creates and updates events in Wix the same way, with your permissions. It never deletes or cancels events.
  • Event exports and the sample file are built in your browser and saved to your computer. They are not sent anywhere.
  • The app's server stores two kinds of records in the app's own collections on your site: links between a row key from your file (the value of your Key column, or the event title when the file has no Key column) and the ID of the event that row created or updated, and a summary of the last import (time, file name and the number of created, updated, linked, skipped and failed rows). It does not store your events or your files.
  • The app's server reads the app plan of your site from Wix.
  • If you ask Aria about your imports, the app's server returns the summary of the last import, the number of linked events and the plan to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and your data stays in your browser and in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Links and the import summary are kept in the app's collections on your site, so that the same file updates the same events next time. The app keeps no copy of your files or events. Removing the app does not change your events. To ask a question about your data, email us.
