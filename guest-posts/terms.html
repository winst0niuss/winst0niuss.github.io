Guest Posts: Write for Us terms and conditions

Last updated: November 16, 2026

These terms apply to your use of the Guest Posts: Write for Us app ("the app") on your Wix site. By installing the app you accept them. Your use of Wix itself is covered by the terms of Wix.

The service

The app adds a guest post form to your site: visitors send a post with their name, email, title, text and optionally a blog category and a cover image. The app checks each post with AI and with your blocked words, and you publish it to your blog, save it as a blog draft or reject it in the app dashboard. If you turn on automatic publishing, posts the AI approves without issues, links or blocked words are published without you. You get an email and a notification about new posts, and the app limits how often posts can be sent.

AI review

The AI review runs through the AI service of the Wix platform and uses your site's AI credits, about one credit per post. It is advice, not a guarantee: it can miss problems or flag good posts. If the check is off, your credits run out or the check does not answer in time, the post waits for you without a verdict.

Your content and your authors

Posts are written by your visitors. You decide what is published, including through automatic publishing, and are responsible for the posts in your blog, for images stored in your Media Manager and for informing authors about how their posts and contact details are used. The spam protection and the AI review reduce, but cannot fully prevent, unwanted posts. Submissions waiting in the app may not be kept after you uninstall it; posts already in your blog stay there.

Plans and price

The app has one paid plan with a free trial. The form shows on your live site and accepts posts only while your trial or plan is active; you can set up everything before that. Plans, prices, trials, billing, renewals and refunds are handled by Wix under its terms, as shown when you upgrade. AI credits are part of your Wix account and are not included in the app plan.
