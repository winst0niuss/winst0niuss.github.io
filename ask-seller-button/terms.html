The service

For each new question it can send you an email and a notification in your dashboard and the Wix app. The app does not take payments, does not change your products or checkout and does not send emails to shoppers: you answer questions yourself.

Emails and limits

Emails to you are sent through Wix and use your site's email balance. The app sends you up to 10 emails an hour; every question is still saved in your dashboard. Delivery is not guaranteed. To protect your site from spam, the app limits how many questions can be sent in a short time. In the editor and preview the button is a sample; the form works on your published site. We do not promise that every theme or page layout shows the button; check your product pages after setup.
