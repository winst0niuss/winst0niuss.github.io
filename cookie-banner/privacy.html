Cookie Banner: GDPR Consent privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Cookie Banner: GDPR Consent ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that asks visitors for consent to cookies. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from visitors

  • The banner loads its look and texts from the app's server on Wix. The request carries only the access token that Wix gives the page, with no name, email or other personal details of the visitor.
  • The visitor's choice is saved by Wix in the visitor's browser and the site's privacy settings, under the terms of Wix. The app does not send the choice to its own server and does not collect or store visitors' names, emails, IP addresses or any other personal data.
  • The visitor's browser keeps the banner settings in its session storage for up to 10 minutes. To tell Google tools the visitor's choice right away on the next page, the banner reads the consent cookie that Wix sets in the visitor's browser. Both stay in the visitor's browser.
  • When the site owner turns on Google Consent Mode, the banner passes the choice to Google tools already on the site through the page's dataLayer. The app itself adds no analytics or advertising tools.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your banner settings — look, texts, categories and your policy link — in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours. Only the app's server can read this collection.
  • The app changes your site's default consent setting when you turn the banner on or off, and reads whether the Wix cookie banner is also on, to warn you about two banners.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix and keeps only whether the Pro plan is active and when it was checked.
  • If you ask Aria about your banner, the app returns its settings for the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Keeping and deleting data

Your settings stay on the site until you change them in the app dashboard. Removing the app takes the banner off your pages and Wix resets the default consent to allowing all cookies; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
