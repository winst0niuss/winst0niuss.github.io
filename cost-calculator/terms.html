The service

The app adds a price calculator to your site. Visitors answer your questions, see an estimate worked out from the prices you set and send you a request with the answers and the estimate. The app saves each request on your site, adds the visitor to your contacts and lets you know by email and a dashboard notification. You can also fill in your calculator yourself and send the quote to a client.

Your prices and your quotes

You set the questions, prices and rules, and you decide what to tell visitors about them. An estimate shown by the calculator is only as accurate as your settings: check your prices and the result in the preview before you publish. The app does not make offers on your behalf and is not part of any contract between you and your clients. You decide which price you finally charge.

Requests and contacts

You run the calculator, decide how to use the details visitors send and are responsible for following the privacy and marketing laws that apply to you. The app shows a box for visitors to agree to be contacted about their quote; with "Show price after contact" this box is always required, because the visitor's details are saved when the price is shown. If you add a webhook, requests are also sent to the service you chose, under that service's terms.

Plans and price

The Free plan includes one calculator on your site with up to 12 questions, requests, contacts and alerts. The paid Pro plan, with a free trial, adds formulas, tiers, named fees and tax, price after contact, a price range, export and webhooks, and more calculators. If Pro ends, a calculator that uses Pro price rules shows the request form without a price, and your requests stay available.
