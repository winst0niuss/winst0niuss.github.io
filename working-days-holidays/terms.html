The service

The app adds actions to the monday.com automation builder that set and count dates in working days: add working days to a date, move a date off weekends and holidays, count working days, and set timelines. It writes only to the columns you choose in each automation and does not take payments or change anything else on your boards.

Holidays and your settings

Working days are calculated from the work week, country, region and company days off set on the app's settings page. Public holidays come from the open date-holidays data set (date-holidays, CC BY-SA 4.0). For Russia, China, Belarus and Uzbekistan the app also applies official government day-off transfers for the years they have been published. Holiday data can be incomplete or change after a government decision, and a date with a time is counted by its UTC date. You are responsible for checking the holidays shown on the settings page and adding company days off where your calendar differs.
