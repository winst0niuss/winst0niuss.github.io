Redirect Checker: 301 & 404 privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Redirect Checker: 301 & 404 ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that checks whether old URLs of a site redirect to the right new pages after a site move. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from site visitors

  • The app has no part on your live site and does not collect any data from site visitors.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The old URLs you paste or upload (a list, a CSV of old and new URLs, or a sitemap) and the expected new URLs are saved in your site, in a private collection of the app, together with the check results. Only the latest check is kept: a new check replaces it.
  • To check a URL, the app's server requests it on your site's own published domains and follows the redirects. It reads the response status, the redirect address and the start of the page head (canonical link and page language). It does not request other domains.
  • The app's server reads your site's published addresses and languages, your existing URL redirects and the app plan of your site from Wix.
  • On the Pro plan, when you click to create redirects, the app's server adds 301 redirects to your site's URL redirect manager in Wix. It never changes or deletes existing redirects.
  • The CSV report is built in your browser and saved to your computer. It is not sent anywhere.
  • If you ask Aria about your redirect check, the app's server returns the summary and the problem URLs of the latest check to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and your data stays in your browser and in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

The latest check stays in your site until you start a new one. Redirects you created stay in your URL redirect manager, where you can edit or delete them, also after you remove the app. To ask a question about your data, email us.
