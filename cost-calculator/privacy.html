Cost Calculator: Price Quotes privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Cost Calculator: Price Quotes ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that lets visitors work out the price of a service and send the site owner a request with the estimate. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from visitors

  • When a visitor sends a request, the app stores their name, email, phone number and comment if they entered them, their answers to the calculator, the estimate, whether they agreed to be contacted, the page language, the time and the request status, in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours. Visitors cannot read this collection; only the site owner's dashboard can see the requests.
  • With "Show price after contact", the visitor's details and answers are saved when the price is shown, even if they do not send the request. The form says so, and the visitor must agree first.
  • The visitor is added to, or updated in, the site's Wix contacts with their name, email and phone. The app does not subscribe them to the site's emails.
  • The request is also passed to Wix Automations on the same site, so the site owner can set up their own automations. If the site owner adds a webhook with the Pro plan, the request, including the visitor's name, email, phone, comment and answers, is sent to the address they chose. Only addresses of Zapier, Make, n8n, Pabbly and IFTTT are accepted; that service then handles the data under its own terms.
  • The site owner decides how to use the requests and is responsible for them. The app does not send emails to visitors.
  • The calculator does not use cookies, tracking or analytics, and does not store visitors' IP addresses. The price is worked out in the visitor's browser, or on the server with "Show price after contact".

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your calculators on the site's own Wix account: questions, prices, texts, design, published versions and your settings, including a webhook address if you add one. Only the app's server can read them.
  • For each new request, the app reads the site owner's email from Wix to send one email about it, and sends a notification to your dashboard. These alerts do not include the visitor's comment. The app does not store your email.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know which features are active.
  • If you ask Aria about your calculators or requests, the app returns your calculators, request counts and totals, and the date, calculator, status and total of recent requests for the current site to Aria inside Wix, without names, emails, phone numbers or comments of your clients. Aria can only read, not change anything.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account in data collections on the site. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Requests stay on the site until you delete them in the app dashboard. Contacts added to Wix contacts stay there under your control. Removing the app takes the calculator off your pages; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collections under its own rules, so save the requests you need first. Visitors who want their data removed can ask the site owner. To ask a question about your data, email us.
