The service

The app adds a button to every page of your site. Visitors open it and change how the site looks for them: text size, spacing, a readable font, contrast, highlighted links and focus, a big cursor, a reading guide and less motion. The changes apply only in that visitor's browser and do not edit your pages. With the Pro plan, the app shows the results of an automatic scan of your published pages run by Wix.

Your responsibility

The app is a tool and not legal advice. It does not fix your pages and does not by itself make your site compliant with WCAG, ADA, the European Accessibility Act or any other law. An automatic scan finds only part of the possible issues. You decide what your site needs, fix your content, write your accessibility statement and check your site with a keyboard and a screen reader.
