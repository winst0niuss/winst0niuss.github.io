The service

The app calculates closing times for Shabbat and the Jewish holidays you choose, for the place you set, and during a closing shows shoppers a notice with the reopening time. If you keep checkout blocking on, the cart and online checkout of your site don't let shoppers place an order during a closing. You can also close or reopen the store manually and add your own closings. The app does not take payments and does not change your products, prices or orders.

Times and your choices

Times are calculated from sunrise and sunset formulas for the coordinates of the place you choose, with the candle lighting minutes, the way Shabbat ends and the holidays you set. They are rounded so the store closes earlier and opens later. Calculated times can differ by a minute or more from a printed calendar, and far north or south, where the sun does not set or it does not get dark enough, they are approximate. You are responsible for choosing settings that follow the custom of your community and for checking the next closings in the dashboard. The app does not provide a halachic ruling. The city list uses GeoNames data (geonames.org, CC BY 4.0).

What is and isn't blocked

Checkout blocking works on your site's online checkout. Orders you create yourself, point of sale, subscription renewals, payments for existing orders and orders created by other apps are not blocked. If the app can't read your settings, it lets orders through rather than block a normal day. We do not promise that every checkout setup, payment method or Wix app is covered; test your checkout after setup.
