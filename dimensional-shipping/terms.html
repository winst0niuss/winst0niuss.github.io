These terms apply to your use of the Dimensional Shipping Rates app ("the app") on your Wix site. By installing the app you accept them. Your use of Wix itself is covered by the terms of Wix.

The service

The app calculates shipping rates at the checkout of your Wix store from the boxes, product sizes and price tables you set up. Each order is packed into your boxes, and each box is priced by its billable weight: its actual weight or its dimensional weight, whichever is higher. Shoppers choose from up to three shipping options you create. The Test a cart tab shows how a cart is packed and what shoppers would pay. Packing is an estimate made by the app; it may differ from how you or your carrier pack an order.

Your settings and your prices

You are responsible for your box sizes, product sizes and weights, divisor, price tables and the rates your shoppers pay, and for checking them with Test a cart before you turn rates on. The app does not book shipments or get prices from carriers, and what your carrier charges you may differ from the rate shown at checkout. If a cart can't be priced, for example a very large order, a checkout in another currency or a country none of your options ships to, checkout shows the backup rate from your Wix shipping settings, so set one in each shipping region where you use the app.

Plans and price

The app has one paid plan, Pro, with a free trial. Without a plan you can set everything up and test carts; your rates are shown at checkout during the trial and on Pro, and otherwise checkout shows your backup rate. Plans, prices, trials, billing, renewals and refunds are handled by Wix under its terms, as shown when you upgrade.
