Scratch Card: Discount Popup privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Scratch Card: Discount Popup ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that shows visitors a scratch card popup and gives subscribers a discount code. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from visitors and subscribers

  • When a visitor signs up, the app stores the fields the site owner asks for (name, email, phone number), whether they agreed to get the site's emails, whether they gave SMS consent by entering a phone number, the discount code they got and what it is worth (with the signature of a random prize, so it is used only once), its expiry date, the page language, the page address and the sign-up time, in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours. Visitors cannot read this collection; only the site owner's dashboard can see the subscribers.
  • For each subscriber the app creates a one-time coupon in the site's store, unless the site owner shows one shared code. If the site owner keeps the email copy on, the app emails the code to the subscriber's address through Wix.
  • If the site owner keeps "Add subscribers to Wix contacts" on, the subscriber is also added to the site's Wix contacts with the label "Scratch card subscriber". The subscriber is subscribed to the site's emails only with the consent the site owner set up; an address that unsubscribed earlier is not subscribed again.
  • When the discount is random, the app gives the visitor's browser a signed note with the prize, so the same visitor keeps the same prize. The browser also remembers when the popup was closed or a code was received, so the popup is not shown again too soon. These notes hold no personal data.
  • The site owner runs the offer, decides how to use subscribers' data and is responsible for it. The app does not send marketing emails or text messages.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics, and does not store visitors' IP addresses.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your popup settings on the site's own Wix account: the discount, form, display rules, design and texts, and a secret key used to sign random prizes. Only the app's server can read them.
  • The app reads the app plan and the site currency from Wix to know whether the popup can show and how to show amounts, and keeps only whether the plan is active and when it was checked.
  • If you ask Aria about your popup, the app returns its settings and subscriber counts to Aria inside Wix, without emails or phone numbers. Aria can only read, not change anything.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Subscribers stay on the site under the site owner's control. Contacts and coupons added to Wix stay there under the site owner's control. Removing the app takes the popup off your pages; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collections under its own rules, so download your subscribers as CSV first. Subscribers who want their data removed can ask the site owner. To ask a question about your data, email us.
