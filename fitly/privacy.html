Fitly Size Chart privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Fitly Size Chart ("Fitly", "we") is an app for Wix sites that shows size charts on product pages. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Data from site owners

  • Size charts you create are stored in a data collection on your own Wix site, not on servers of ours.
  • The app reads your product and category names and SKUs through Wix to let you choose where a chart shows.
  • The app reads your app plan from Wix to apply plan limits. It does not store it.

Data from site visitors

Fitly does not collect or store personal data of your visitors. Measurements entered in Find my size are used in the visitor's browser to suggest a size and are never sent or saved.

We do not use cookies or browser storage, we do not track visitors, we do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and your data stays in your Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

You can view, change, export to CSV or delete your size charts in the app at any time. Before removing the app, export the charts you want to keep: Wix warns that removing an app also removes its data. To ask about your data, email us, and we will answer within 30 days.
