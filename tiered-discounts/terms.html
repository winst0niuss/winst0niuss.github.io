The service

You set up to five discount tiers by the number of items or by the cart amount, for all products or for products you choose. The app creates one automatic discount in your store for each tier and shows a window with the shopper's progress in the corner of your site pages and, if you add it, a block on the cart page. The window's button opens checkout or the cart page. The app does not take payments and does not change your products, prices, stock or orders.
