Last updated: November 16, 2026

These terms apply to your use of the Event Submissions & Approval app ("the app") on your Wix site. By installing the app you accept them. Your use of Wix itself is covered by the terms of Wix.

The service

The app adds an event submission form to your site: visitors send an event with their name and email, the event name, start and end time, a venue and address or an online event, a description and optionally a link and an image. The app checks each submission with AI, with date checks and with your blocked words, and you publish it to your Wix Events calendar, save it as a Wix Events draft or reject it in the app dashboard. If you turn on automatic publishing, events the AI approves without issues, links or blocked words are published without you. You get an email and a notification about new submissions, and the app limits how often events can be sent.

AI review

The AI review runs through the AI service of the Wix platform and uses your site's AI credits, about one credit per submission. It is advice, not a guarantee: it can miss problems or flag good events. If the check is off, your credits run out or the check does not answer in time, the event waits for you without a verdict.

Your content and your organizers

Events are submitted by your visitors. You decide what is published, including through automatic publishing, and are responsible for the events in your calendar, their accuracy, images stored in your Media Manager and for informing organizers about how their events and contact details are used. Times are taken in the time zone of the visitor who submits the event; check them before publishing. The spam protection and the AI review reduce, but cannot fully prevent, unwanted submissions. Submissions waiting in the app may not be kept after you uninstall it; events already in Wix Events stay there.

Plans and price

The app has one paid plan with a free trial. The form shows on your live site and accepts events only while your trial or plan is active; you can set up everything before that. Plans, prices, trials, billing, renewals and refunds are handled by Wix under its terms, as shown when you upgrade. AI credits are part of your Wix account and are not included in the app plan. Wix Events is a separate Wix app and is needed to publish events.
