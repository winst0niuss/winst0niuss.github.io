The service

You choose one of your store products as a gift and an amount. The app creates an automatic discount in your store that makes one gift free when the rest of the cart reaches the amount, and shows a block with a progress bar in the side cart and, if you add it, on the cart page. The block can add the gift to the cart and remove it when the cart drops below the amount. The app does not take payments and does not change your products, prices, stock or orders.
