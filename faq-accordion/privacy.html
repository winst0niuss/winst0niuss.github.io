FAQ Accordion: Page & Product privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

FAQ Accordion: Page & Product ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that shows questions and answers as an accordion on site pages and on product pages. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from visitors

  • An FAQ block loads its questions, answers and look settings from the app's server on Wix. On a product page the request carries only the ID of the product being viewed, so the server can pick the sets meant for it. The app does not collect or store visitors' names, emails, IP addresses or any other personal data.
  • To load faster, the visitor's browser keeps the shown FAQ in its own session storage for up to 5 minutes. This stays in the visitor's browser and contains only the questions and settings shown on the page.
  • The search box filters questions inside the visitor's browser; search words are not sent anywhere.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your FAQ sets, questions, answers, product rules and look settings in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours. Only the app's server can read this collection.
  • To match sets to products, the app reads product names, IDs and categories or collections from Wix Stores (Read Products permissions). It does not change products.
  • When you choose to import, the app reads the categories, questions and answers of the Wix FAQ app on your site (Read FAQ permission) and copies them into its own sets. It does not change or delete anything in the Wix FAQ app.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know whether the FAQ should show on the live site, and keeps only whether the plan is active and when it was checked.
  • If you ask Aria about your FAQ, the app returns the sets, their product rules and questions of the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Your FAQ stays on the site until you change or delete it in the app dashboard. Removing the app takes the FAQ blocks off your pages; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
