Price by Size Calculator privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Price by Size Calculator ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that calculates product prices from a size entered by a shopper. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Data from site owners

  • Pricing rules you create are stored in a data collection on your own Wix site, not on servers of ours.
  • The app reads your product names, prices and variants through Wix to let you choose products and to calculate prices.
  • The app reads your app plan from Wix to apply plan limits. It does not store it.

Data from shoppers

The size, length, weight or text a shopper enters is sent to the app to calculate the price and is added to that shopper's Wix cart, where it becomes part of the order you receive. The app does not store it separately.

To check the cart and checkout, Wix gives the app access to checkout data, which can include cart items, shipping address and contact details. The app uses only the items in the cart for this check and does not store or share any of this data.

We do not use cookies or browser storage, we do not track visitors, we do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and your data stays in your Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

You can view, change or delete your rules in the app at any time. Orders and carts are managed in your Wix account. Wix warns that removing an app also removes its data. To ask about your data, email us, and we will answer within 30 days.
