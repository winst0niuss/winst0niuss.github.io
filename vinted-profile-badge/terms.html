The service

The app shows a badge on your site with your Vinted shop name, a link to your Vinted profile and a Follow on Vinted button. On the paid plan it also shows your star rating and number of reviews. The app reads only the public profile at the link you enter, about once a day. It does not log in to Vinted, does not need your Vinted password and does not change anything on Vinted.

Your profile and data from Vinted

Enter only a link to a Vinted profile that you own or are allowed to show. Vinted has no official interface for this data, so it may be late, change format or stop answering at any time. When Vinted does not answer, the badge keeps the last data it got for up to 30 days, or shows the rating and number of reviews you entered yourself. You are responsible for numbers you enter. We do not promise that the rating is always current or that the badge always shows it.
