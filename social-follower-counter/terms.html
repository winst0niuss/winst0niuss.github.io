The service

The app shows a card on your site with a YouTube channel, its subscriber count and a link to the channel.

Numbers on the card

Data comes from YouTube and updates about once a day. YouTube rounds counts down to three significant figures. We do not promise that a number is current or exact, and YouTube may change or stop its public data at any time. If an update fails, the card shows the last data for up to 30 days, then disappears until an update succeeds.

Plans and payment

The card is free for channels with fewer than 10,000 subscribers. Channels with 10,000 subscribers or more need the paid plan, with a 7-day free trial. You can set up the card without a plan. If your channel reaches 10,000 subscribers without a plan, or the plan ends, the card disappears from your site and your settings stay saved.
