Free Shipping Bar: Cart Goal privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Free Shipping Bar: Cart Goal ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix stores that shows shoppers how much more they need to add to the cart for free shipping, at the top of the site pages, in the side cart and on the cart page. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from shoppers

  • The top bar gets its settings with the page from Wix, and the cart bars load the bar settings from the app's server on Wix. These requests carry no shopper data, and the app does not collect or store shoppers' names, emails, IP addresses or any other personal data.
  • In the shopper's browser, the bars read the current cart (item prices, quantities, whether an item ships, and the currency) to show how much is left. The cart stays in Wix; the app does not copy it to its server and does not change it.
  • At most once per browsing session, the top bar may ask the app's server to check that the store's plan is still active. The request carries no shopper data.
  • The shopper's browser keeps the bar settings for up to 5 minutes and remembers, for the browsing session, whether the shopper closed the top bar. This stays in the shopper's browser session storage and contains no personal data.
  • Checkout, payments, shipping and orders are handled by Wix; the app does not see or store buyers' details or payment information.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app reads your store's shipping rates and delivery regions (Read eCommerce permission) to find the amount of your free shipping rate, and keeps that amount and the chosen region.
  • The app reads your site's basic info (Read site basic info permission), such as the site currency and language, to show amounts and texts correctly.
  • The app stores your bar settings in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours: where the amount comes from, the amount, the places where the bar shows, the color and your texts. Only the app's server can read this collection.
  • The app adds a script to your site (Manage Embedded Scripts permission) that carries the bar settings, so the top bar shows without extra requests. The app updates it when you save settings or your plan changes.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know whether the bar should show on the live site, and keeps only whether the plan is active and when it was checked.
  • If you ask Aria about your free shipping bar, the app returns the bar settings of the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Settings stay on the site until you change them in the app dashboard. Removing the app takes the bars off your site, and Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
