The service

On the products you choose, the app shows a Request a price button instead of the price and the buy buttons, collects price requests from shoppers and shows them in your dashboard. The cart and checkout do not accept those products. The app does not take payments and does not send emails to shoppers: you answer requests yourself.

Your requests and shoppers

You are responsible for answering requests, for the prices you offer and for handling shoppers' contact details under the laws that apply to you. Delete requests you no longer need in the app dashboard.

Limits

To protect your site from spam, the app limits how many requests can be sent in a short time. Product galleries keep showing prices unless you hide them in the gallery settings. We do not promise that every theme or page layout hides the price; check your product pages after setup.
