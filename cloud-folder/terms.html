The service

The app shows the files of a Dropbox folder you share by link as a file library on your site pages: visitors can browse subfolders, search, sort, open and download files. The app reads the folder through the Dropbox API using the share link you paste; it does not sign in to your Dropbox account and does not change, upload or delete anything in Dropbox. The app is independent and is not made or endorsed by Wix or Dropbox.

Your content

You are responsible for the files in the folders you show, for having the right to share them, and for who can open the share link. Anyone with your Dropbox share link can see the folder in Dropbox; the app itself never shows the link to visitors. A folder set to site members only is shown to logged-in members of your site; a file opened by a member is delivered to that member's browser.
