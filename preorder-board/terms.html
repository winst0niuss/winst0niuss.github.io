The service

The app keeps a board of your pre-order products. For each product you enter the incoming batches with their arrival dates and quantities and a pre-order start date. The app lines up approved orders placed since that date first come, first served, so earlier orders get earlier batches, and shows the queue with each order's batch date. On the product page it can show new shoppers a "Pre-order · Ships by" date taken from the next batch with free units. It can email buyers their ship date from your template, alert you by email and dashboard notification when pre-orders exceed the units on the way, and export the queue as a CSV file.

Your dates and your emails

You are responsible for the batches, dates and quantities you enter. They are your estimates, and every date the app shows or emails is calculated only from them and from your orders. The product page shows a date only when your batches still have free units. You are responsible for the content of your email templates, for choosing which buyers get an email, and for having the right to email your buyers about their orders. Review the queue before you send emails.
