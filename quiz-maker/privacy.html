Quiz & Assessment Builder privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Quiz & Assessment Builder ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that shows trivia and personality quizzes on the site's pages and can ask visitors for a name and email before the result. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from visitors

  • The quiz loads from the app's server on Wix. Answers are counted in the visitor's browser and are not stored.
  • Only if the site owner turns on contact collection for a quiz, the visitor is asked for their email, and optionally a name and consent, before seeing the result. The app then stores the name, email, the result and the score with the date, in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours. Only the site owner's dashboard can read these contacts. If the same email takes the same quiz again within a day, the existing contact is updated instead of adding a new one.
  • The site owner decides how to use these contacts and is responsible for it, including the consent text shown to visitors. The app does not send emails to visitors.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics, and does not store visitors' IP addresses.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your quizzes in a data collection on the site's own Wix account: questions, answers, results, button links, contact form settings and colors. Only the app's server can read this collection.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know which quizzes can show on the live site and whether contacts can be collected, and keeps only whether the plan is active and when it was checked.
  • If you ask Aria about your quizzes, the app returns the list of quizzes of the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Quizzes and contacts stay on the site until you change or delete them in the app dashboard. Deleting a quiz deletes its contacts. Removing the app takes the quizzes off your pages; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collections under its own rules. Visitors who want their contact removed can ask the site owner. To ask a question about your data, email us.
