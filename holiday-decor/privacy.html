Christmas: Snow & Sale Bar privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Christmas: Snow & Sale Bar ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that adds holiday effects, a garland of lights and a sale bar with a countdown to the site's pages. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from visitors

  • The decorations get their settings from the page itself. The app does not collect or store visitors' names, emails, IP addresses or any other personal data.
  • When a visitor closes the sale bar, that choice is kept in the visitor's browser (session storage) until the end of the visit, so the bar doesn't come back on the next pages. It never leaves the browser.
  • Once in a while, when Pro features are on, the script on the page asks the app's server to check that the plan is still active. The request carries no names, emails or other personal data of the visitor.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your decoration settings — theme or holiday calendar, dates, effect, garland, sale bar text, button, link and end time — and your plan status in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours. Only the app's server can read this collection.
  • The app reads basic site information and the app plan from Wix to know whether Pro is active, and adds its script to your site's pages through Wix.
  • If you ask Aria about your decorations, the app returns their settings for the current site to Aria inside Wix. Aria can only read, not change anything.

The app does not send emails or notifications. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Keeping and deleting data

Your settings stay on the site until you change them in the app dashboard. Removing the app in your site's Manage Apps takes the decorations off your pages; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collection under its own rules. To ask a question about your data, email us.
