Poll & Vote: Product Waitlist privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Poll & Vote: Product Waitlist ("the app", "we") is an app for Wix sites that shows polls, voting boards and trivia quizzes, and collects a launch waitlist for the site owner. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Data from visitors

  • When a visitor votes, the app stores the chosen option, the time and a one-way hash of the visitor or member ID that Wix gives the app, so the same visitor cannot vote twice. The hash cannot be turned back into the ID, and the app stores no names, emails or IP addresses with votes.
  • When a visitor joins the waitlist, the app stores the fields the site owner turned on (name, email, phone and the answer to the owner's question), the option they voted for, the page language and the time, in a data collection on the site's own Wix account and not on servers of ours. Visitors cannot read these collections; only the site owner's dashboard can see the waitlist.
  • If the site owner keeps "Add signups to your Wix contacts" on, the person is also added to the site's Wix contacts with the label "Product waitlist". The app does not subscribe anyone to the site's emails.
  • The visitor's browser remembers that it already joined the waitlist of a poll, so the form is not shown again. This note holds no personal data.
  • The site owner runs the poll and the waitlist, decides how to use the data and is responsible for it. The app does not send emails to visitors.
  • The app does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Data from site owners

  • The app stores your polls on the site's own Wix account: questions, options (text, images, or products of your store with their name, photo and page link), translations, dates, design and texts. Only the app's server can read them; visitors get only what the poll shows.
  • The app reads the app plan from Wix to know whether polls can show on the live site, and keeps only whether the plan is active and when it was checked.
  • If you ask Aria about your polls, the app returns their questions, status, vote and signup counts and the leading option to Aria inside Wix. It does not share contacts. Aria can only read, not change anything.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where data is processed

The app runs on Wix hosting, and data stays in the site's Wix account. Wix acts as our service provider. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Retention, access and deletion

Votes and waitlist signups stay on the site until you delete them. You can delete a single signup in the app dashboard, for example when a visitor asks you to remove their data; resetting votes deletes the votes of a poll, and you can also delete its waitlist; deleting a poll deletes its votes and waitlist. Contacts added to Wix contacts stay there under your control. Removing the app takes the polls off your pages; Wix may keep or delete the app's data collections under its own rules, so export your waitlist as CSV first. Visitors who want their data removed can ask the site owner. To ask a question about your data, email us.
