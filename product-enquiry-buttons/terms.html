The service

The app adds three buttons to your product pages, and you choose where each one appears. Make an offer lets shoppers offer a price, and you accept it, decline it or answer with another price, yourself or by automatic rules you set. An accepted offer is bought through a personal link, for one item at the agreed price and for a limited time. Ask a question lets shoppers send you a question about a product. Request a price hides the price and the buy buttons on the products you choose and lets shoppers ask for the price instead; the cart and checkout don't accept these products.
